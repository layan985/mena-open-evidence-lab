## Research discipline

Do not publish:
- a country ranking from insufficient vintage histories;
- a composite 'quality score' combining revision size and archive accessibility;
- a narrative-reversal rate from cherry-picked cases;
- any claim that a revision equals an error or manipulation.

## Commercial discipline

Do not pitch generic 'data analysis'. Lead with one specific failure mode:

**Historical evaluation can accidentally use data that was not available at the historical cutoff.**

Ask buyers for one country, one indicator/model and one historical date. Sell the bounded diagnostic before a large project.
