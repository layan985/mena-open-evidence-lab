# Prioritized Research Backlog

## Tier 1 — Build now

### A. Saudi GDP full vintage chain
Reconstruct annual and quarterly releases around the comprehensive national-accounts revision. Measure revisions, identify narrative reversals and preserve the exact source manifest.

### B. Archive Reconstructability pilot
Test the six ARG conditions on Saudi Arabia, Morocco, Tunisia and Jordan. Publish missing historical evidence as a result rather than filling gaps by inference.

### C. Claim Survival pilot
Sample before outcome coding. Begin with 20 pilot candidates to test coding rules, then freeze the 100-claim sampling process.

## Tier 2 — High-value extensions

### D. Denominator Shock Atlas
Track cases where an unchanged label masks a changed population, age range, citizenship group, sector coverage or denominator. Quantify when rankings/conclusions change solely because the denominator differs.

### E. Revision Calendar
Measure publication lag and revision timing by indicator/statistical office. Ask how long a first estimate should be treated as provisional based on observed revision history.

### F. Hindsight Contamination Audit
Take published forecasting/model-evaluation exercises that use macro data and determine whether latest-vintage series leak future revisions into historical tests.

### G. Derived-Ratio Revision Propagation
Measure how GDP revisions propagate mechanically into debt/GDP, deficit/GDP, productivity and per-capita ratios even when the numerator is unchanged.

## Tier 3 — Big-paper candidates after infrastructure matures

### H. Does measurement disagreement predict revision?
Combine official first releases with independent physical/high-frequency proxies. Test whether unusually large disagreement predicts subsequent official revisions without assuming either measurement is the truth.

### I. Revision-aware forecasting benchmark
Compare model rankings using first-release targets versus latest-vintage targets. Quantify how often the 'best model' changes depending on which vintage defines truth.

### J. Regional reproducibility audit
Sample high-impact public MENA economic charts/claims and measure the share that can be reconstructed from still-accessible source records.

## Selection rule

A project moves upward only if it creates at least two of:

- new defensible knowledge;
- reusable public infrastructure;
- external-validation opportunity;
- clear buyer use case;
- strong institutional distribution path.
