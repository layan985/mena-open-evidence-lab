# UNWDF 2026 — Meeting Card

## What we do
MENA Evidence Lab reconstructs the official economic information that existed at a historical date, preserves later revisions and methodology changes, and tests whether economic conclusions survive them.

## One example
Saudi Arabia's official 2023 real-GDP growth appears as −0.8% in the preserved pre-comprehensive-revision vintage and +0.5% in the revised national accounts. We preserve both and the transition between them.

## What we are building
- historical vintage archive;
- revision-exposure measures;
- archive-reconstructability grades;
- methodology-shock registry;
- pre-registered Claim Survival study.

## What we need
