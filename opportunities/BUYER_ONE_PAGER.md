# MENA Evidence Lab — Historical Data & Revision Audit

## The problem

A historical model, forecast evaluation or policy analysis can look perfectly reproducible and still use information that did not exist at the historical date being evaluated.

Official economic statistics are revised. Base years change. Coverage expands. Historical releases disappear. Latest-vintage databases can silently rewrite the information set available to the original analyst.

## What we test

Give the Lab:

- a country or market;
- the indicators/model involved;
- the historical cutoff or evaluation window;
- the current analysis or output being relied on.

We reconstruct the official information set that was actually available, then compare the conclusion with the latest comparable data.

## What you receive

- dated historical release/vintage manifest;
- original values recoverable at the cutoff;
- latest comparable values;
- methodology/base-year change log;
- source files/links and hashes where available;
- reproducible transformation record;
- revision/look-ahead impact on the relevant conclusion or model evaluation;
- concise limitations and decision memo.

## Typical use cases

- macro forecast backtests;
- investment or country-risk research;
- historical policy evaluations;
- research replications;
- dashboards built from changing official series;
- cross-country indicator comparisons;
- due diligence on a headline economic claim.

## Public proof

The Lab's public MENA Vintage Vault preserves official revisions rather than overwriting old values. Its first published Saudi case records a pre-comprehensive-revision 2023 real-GDP growth estimate of −0.8% and a later revised estimate of +0.5%, with the source and methodology transition retained.

## Scope

A bounded one-country / one-indicator historical reconstruction typically starts at **US$1,500**. Larger revision/look-ahead audits are scoped after inspecting the series, historical depth and model perimeter.

No integration or long consulting programme is required for the initial diagnostic.
