# Institutional Target Map

## Tier A — Capability / evidence access

### National statistical offices
GASTAT, Jordan Department of Statistics, HCP Morocco, INS Tunisia, GCC-STAT and additional MENA NSOs as the reconstruction expands.

**Reason:** primary releases, methodology notes, revision policy and technical validation.

**Ask:** archived source or a precise technical check, not generic partnership.

### PARIS21
**Reason:** statistical-system capacity, archiving/documentation history, Data & AI work, direct relevance to NSO practice.

**Ask:** technical routing and critique of the measurement framework.

### Global Partnership for Sustainable Development Data
**Reason:** official-statistics/data-system network and distribution into data-for-development practitioners.

**Ask:** network fit and routing to relevant official-statistics/data infrastructure work.

### Open Data Watch
**Reason:** statistical openness and accessibility are adjacent to archive reconstructability.

**Ask:** methodological comparison between current openness measures and historical recoverability.

## Tier B — Regional distribution / applied use

### UN ESCWA statistics/data teams
**Reason:** regional statistical comparability and institutional access.

**Ask:** identify a bounded regional use case or review candidate indicators.

### UN Statistics Division / UN World Data Forum
**Reason:** global official-statistics community and November 2026 Riyadh convening.

**Ask:** participation/technical exchange around historical information sets and revision reproducibility.

### IMF / World Bank data and macro-statistics teams
**Reason:** large downstream users and disseminators of national data; real-time/revision issues matter for surveillance and research.

**Ask:** technical user feedback or one candidate historical-series use case.

## Tier C — Revenue users

### Macro consultancies and forecasting teams
**Reason:** historical models/backtests can be sensitive to revised data.

**Ask:** one bounded historical cutoff/model to audit.

### Development/evaluation contractors
**Reason:** reports often assemble fragmented multi-country indicators and need defensible provenance.

**Ask:** subcontracted indicator/provenance audit or evidence-engineering package.

### Banks / country-risk / investment research
**Reason:** narrative, threshold and forecast decisions depend on real-time macro information.

**Ask:** paid one-country reconstruction or revision audit.

## Tier D — Reputation multipliers

### Data/economics journalists
**Reason:** verified revision or comparability findings are inherently explainable and sourceable.

**Ask:** none initially; provide the evidence package.

### Reproducibility researchers
**Reason:** independent checking converts internal quality claims into demonstrated credibility.

**Ask:** reproduce one randomly chosen case.

## Time allocation

Until the first multi-country release exists:

- 40% primary-source/research build;
- 25% paid prospecting/proposals;
- 20% technical validators/institutional routes;
- 10% public communication;
- 5% broad networking.

Do not reverse this allocation to chase visibility before evidence exists.
