# MENA Evidence Lab — Revenue Conversion Pipeline

## Commercial principle

Sell a decision-risk diagnosis, not 'research support'. The buyer should know exactly what uncertainty will be reduced and what evidence object they receive.

## Entry product 1 — Historical Information-Set Reconstruction

**Trigger:** A model, paper, investment memo or policy evaluation claims to represent what was knowable at a historical date.

**Question:** Did the analysis use only information that actually existed then?

**Deliverables:**
- historical cutoff manifest;
- original official releases/vintages;
- exact values known by the cutoff;
- methodology regime;
- source archive and hashes;
- differences versus latest-vintage data;
- short implications memo.

**Price test:** US$1,500–5,000.

**Fast sales test:** Offer a bounded one-country / one-indicator pilot with a fixed written perimeter.

## Entry product 2 — Revision & Look-Ahead Audit

**Trigger:** A forecasting/backtesting/model team evaluates history using today's macro database.

**Question:** How much of the reported performance or historical conclusion survives real-time vintages?

**Deliverables:**
- hindsight-contamination map;
- first-release vs later-vintage comparison;
- performance/conclusion rerun;
- routine-revision vs methodology-shock decomposition;
- robustness note.

**Price test:** US$2,500–7,500.

## Entry product 3 — Indicator & Provenance Audit

**Trigger:** A development/evaluation/research project depends on a regional indicator table assembled from fragmented sources.

**Question:** Are definitions, denominators and transformations sufficiently comparable to support the intended claim?

**Deliverables:**
- indicator contract;
- source and transformation register;
- comparability matrix;
- exceptions and missingness log;
- claim-level QA;
- reproducible delivery package.

**Price test:** US$5,000–12,000.

## Entry product 4 — Evidence Engineering Work Package

**Trigger:** A visualization, dashboard, report or data product needs a defensible analytical layer behind it.

**Question:** Can every material output resolve to a frozen dataset, reproducible transformation and validation check?

**Deliverables:**
- frozen input manifest;
- data QA;
- chart-ready analytical tables;
- validation tests;
- exceptions register;
- handover documentation.

**Commercial route:** subcontract to design/data-engineering primes or contract directly with research organizations.

## Prospect qualification

Prioritize an organization if at least two are true:

- publishes or sells macro forecasts;
- maintains historical economic databases;
- evaluates policies/programmes using dated evidence;
- produces high-stakes regional dashboards/reports;
- compares indicators across MENA countries;
- runs model backtests using macro data;
- has a client deliverable that requires traceable source QA.

Do not over-invest in prospects whose problem is simply 'we need a dashboard' unless a qualified technical prime needs the Lab's evidence layer.

## Sales message anatomy

1. Show one concrete failure mode.
2. State what can be tested with no integration.
3. Ask for the smallest data/input needed.
4. Offer a fixed perimeter and written output.
5. Do not promise a finding before inspecting the data.

## Commercial experiment ledger

For every proposal/outreach record:

- organization;
- buyer role;
- observed trigger;
- product pitched;
- price;
- date;
- response;
- objection;
- outcome;
- days to decision;
- follow-up date.

After 20 qualified pitches, update positioning and price from actual objections/conversion rather than intuition.
