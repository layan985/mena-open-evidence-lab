# Funder Case — Preserve the Statistical Record

## The public-good problem

Official economic statistics are not static facts. Initial estimates are revised; methods are rebased; classifications change; coverage improves. Yet historical releases can become difficult to retrieve, making it impossible to reproduce the information set available when a policy decision, forecast or public claim was made.

## The proposed public infrastructure

MENA Evidence Lab preserves historical releases as evidence objects linked to dates, values, methodology regimes, source records and reproducible transformations.

The Lab then measures two different properties separately:

- how much comparable estimates change after publication;
- how completely the historical information set remains recoverable.

This creates infrastructure for reproducible research, responsible journalism and more defensible retrospective policy analysis.

## What funding unlocks

A bounded grant should purchase measurable public goods:

- recovered and preserved historical releases;
- validated machine-readable observations;
- source and methodology manifests;
- independent double checking;
- open methods/code;
- training/reproduction exercises;
- stable public documentation.

## What funding does not purchase

- predetermined findings;
- sponsor control over conclusions;
- inflated country rankings from incomplete evidence;
- hidden proprietary ownership of publicly funded source reconstructions.

## Proof of seriousness

The Lab already maintains a Vintage Vault, Data Revision Atlas specification, Methodology Shock Registry, validation standards and a first Saudi narrative-reversal evidence case. The next phase turns that foundation into comparable multi-country research with explicit release gates.

## Long-run model

Public infrastructure remains inspectable. Commissioned historical reconstructions, revision audits, provenance audits and evidence-engineering assignments provide a complementary revenue stream for bounded institutional problems.

Funding therefore accelerates the reusable public layer rather than substituting indefinitely for a business model.
