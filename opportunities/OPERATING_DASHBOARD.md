# Operating Dashboard

## Research production
- verified vintage observations
- complete historical source pairs/chains
- methodology shocks documented
- archive gaps documented
- claims sampled / coded / double-coded
- external checks completed
- corrections opened / closed

## Use and reputation
- dataset downloads or documented uses
- citations / substantive external references
- technical reviewer responses
- institutional requests for data/methods
- workshop/reproduction completions

## Commercial
- qualified prospects contacted
- positive replies
- scoped calls
- proposals
- paid engagements
- booked revenue
- realized revenue
- average contract value
- days from first contact to decision
- repeat clients

## Opportunity pipeline
- active tenders/grants
- deadline
- fit score
- route: prime / subcontract / grant / partnership / conference
- next action
- outcome

## Anti-vanity rule
Follower counts, impressions and generic connections can be recorded, but they do not enter the core scorecard unless they produce identifiable evidence use, qualified inbound, citation, validation, partnership or revenue.
