# Partnership Scorecard

Score a potential institutional relationship before spending founder time.

## +2 each
- Can unlock primary-source or historical archive access.
- Can provide serious technical review/validation.
- Can commission or subcontract paid evidence work.
- Can distribute a verified result to a highly relevant professional audience.
- Has an active programme directly touching official statistics, provenance, reproducibility or regional data systems.

## +1 each
- Can introduce a relevant national statistical office or data team.
- Can host a technical workshop or reproduction exercise.
- Can contribute a complementary dataset or methodology.
- Has a credible route to future grant/contract collaboration.

## −2 each
- Relationship is mostly symbolic/logo collection.
- Requires substantial unpaid bespoke work before identifying a concrete use.
- Expects promotional claims the evidence cannot support.
- Has no identifiable person/team owning the relevant problem.

## Priority
- **8+**: direct targeted approach.
- **5–7**: engage after one more evidence object is ready.
- **2–4**: low-touch follow/future route.
- **≤1**: do not spend active outreach time.

The point is to accumulate capability, evidence access, distribution and revenue—not partner logos.
