## Compounding loop

1. Recover one evidence object.
2. Publish it with strict claim boundaries.
3. Invite independent validation.
4. Extract the generalizable failure mode.
5. Offer a bounded diagnostic for organizations exposed to that failure mode.
6. Feed new non-confidential methodological learning back into the public system.

The Lab should not invent separate marketing research. The strongest sales evidence is the public research itself.
