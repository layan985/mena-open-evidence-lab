# Do Not Chase

Until the flagship measurement work is released, deprioritize:

- broad unpaid 'partnership' meetings with no technical owner or use case;
- generic incubators whose main output is pitch coaching;
- content collaborations that do not cite/link the evidence;
- enormous regional datasets without a specific research question;
- dashboards that duplicate already available official-data portals;
- premature subscription products before repeated buyer demand exists;
- composite institutional rankings built from thin historical evidence;
- vanity speaking opportunities that require substantial unpaid preparation but create no evidence access, buyer route or relevant distribution.

The opportunity cost is primary-source reconstruction, validation and paid diagnostics.
