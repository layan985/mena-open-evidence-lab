# Governance

Five operating roles: Founder & Research Director; Data Systems Lead; Arabic-English Narrative Methods Lead; Economic Measurement Lead; Verification & Public Outputs Lead.

Authorship follows substantive contribution, not title. Dataset contributors are credited through release contributor records and software contributors through version control.

Material methodology changes affecting published outputs require a public issue or decision note. Changes to scoring, missingness, source inclusion, or verification rules cannot be made silently.

No analyst approves their own work. A record may be marked `verified` only after a second person or independent retrieval path confirms the value and source metadata.

Funding, advisory roles, and institutional partnerships must be disclosed on the relevant release.
