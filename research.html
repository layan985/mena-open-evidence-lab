Research

Four programs. Each output must resolve to evidence.

The Lab separates research questions from publication formats. Programs define the durable analytical agenda; releases, briefs, datasets, replications and commissioned outputs are versioned objects inside that agenda.

Program 01

Macroeconomic Measurement & Official Statistics

Official releases, revisions, vintages, comparability, price measurement and narrative/market-stress monitoring.

Program 02

Public Finance & Procurement

Procurement awards, plans, supplier structure, donor opportunities, public-spending evidence and source provenance.

Program 03

Firms, Labor & Technology

Firm-level technology adoption, labor adjustment, disclosure coding and regional business evidence.

Program 04

Methods & Reproducibility

Replication protocols, second coding, audit trails, causal/econometric methods and reproducible publication infrastructure.

Publication rule

A research program is not evidence by itself. Material claims must point to a versioned release or publication with source, method, code/transformations where applicable, QA, limitations and validation state.
