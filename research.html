Research

Durable research programmes, not a pile of disconnected outputs.

The Lab organizes research around recurring economic and measurement problems. Publications, datasets, code, replications and methods records sit underneath those programmes and inherit explicit evidence states.

Active programme

Macroeconomic Conditions

Inflation and household purchasing power, monetary transmission, FX stress, fiscal conditions, external balances, financial conditions, energy/import exposure and country-risk indicators.

Current Lab objects

Economic Narrative & Market Stress ObservatoryOfficial Price Release ObservatoryEvidence Terminal

Research agenda. The programme is designed to support revision-aware monitoring and careful interpretation of official macroeconomic releases. Example questions in the programme roadmap include when headline aggregates conceal important category-level pressure, how exchange-rate episodes transmit into price evidence, and how release timing changes what was knowable at a given date.

Programme rule

A programme is an agenda, not proof. A material claim must resolve to a named, versioned research object whose data, sources, method, QA, limitations, review state and correction path can be inspected.
