Latest finding · Evidence Brief 001

Jordan’s headline inflation accelerated through May, but monthly pressure had already cooled by June.

Official Jordan Department of Statistics releases · January–June 2026 · retrieved 13 August 2026.

Annual headline CPI rose from 1.06% in January to 2.83% in May before easing to 2.79% in June. The month-on-month rate was only 0.14% in June, down from 0.76% in March and 0.70% in April. This is a descriptive release-based finding, not a welfare or causal claim.

Programme 03

Public Statistics, Definitions & Revisions

What did an official indicator mean at the moment it was released, how has that meaning changed, and can another researcher reconstruct the answer later?

Published · Data Note v1.0

When does the same unemployment rate stop meaning the same thing?

A source-linked comparison of Jordan, Egypt and Morocco finds that all three systems center unemployment measurement on a 15+ framework, while differing materially in population perimeter and classification architecture. Egypt explicitly targets the Egyptian civilian labour force inside the state; Jordan’s releases distinguish Jordanian and non-Jordanian labour-market populations; Morocco’s redesigned 2026 survey separates strict unemployment from a potential labour force outside it.
