Research

Research designed around evidence that can survive inspection.

Our work focuses on economic questions where regional evidence is fragmented across institutions, vintages, definitions and languages. Each project is developed with explicit source provenance, reproducible analysis and stated limits on what the evidence can support.

Programme rule

A research programme is an agenda, not proof. A material claim must resolve to a named, versioned research object whose data, sources, method, QA, limitations, review state and correction path can be inspected.
