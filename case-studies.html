Commissioned work · Engagement 001

Regional Jobs & Resilience Evidence Review

The decision

A confidential client required a defensible evidence architecture for a regional jobs and resilience question.

Initial evidence

Multiple source classes, programme records and contextual indicators with different provenance and analytical strength.

Problem

The analytical risk was not simply missing data; it was combining evidence with different denominators, source quality, attrition patterns and identification strength as if they were equivalent.

What the Lab did

Reconstructed source provenance, checked programme-data quality, documented attrition treatment, separated descriptive and causal evidence, and built an allocation-sensitivity layer.

Finding

Client-specific findings and recommendations remain confidential. The public record documents the analytical architecture and disclosure boundary rather than inventing a public outcome.

Decision implication

The public case record demonstrates how evidence quality and comparability are surfaced before a recommendation is allowed to carry decision weight.

Limitations

No client-specific data, sensitive recommendation, attributed outcome or confidential document is published without explicit rights.
