# Apply to MODERN Cohort 01

MODERN is the research-production network of the MENA Open Data & Evidence Lab.

Applications are reviewed on demonstrated research judgment, reproducibility, reliability, and fit — not prestige or membership interest.

## Application route

Because applications contain personal contact information and unpublished work, **do not submit an application as a public GitHub issue**.

Email your application to **layanoraidi@gmail.com** with subject:

`MODERN Cohort 01 Application — [Your Name]`

Attach or link one PDF/Markdown document containing:

1. Full name
2. University / institution
3. Degree or role
4. Country / time zone
5. Email
6. GitHub or portfolio, if available
7. Up to three preferred research pods
8. A 150-word answer: *What MENA research question would you most like to answer, and why?*
9. Tools you can currently use: Python / R / Stata / SQL / Git / LaTeX / other
10. One example of analytical, coding, research, or writing work
11. Whether you can contribute roughly 4–6 hours/week from September 2026 through January 2027
12. Whether you want to be considered for one of 11 Research Fellowship places

## Required mini reproducibility task

Submit a public repository using a **lawfully accessible MENA-relevant public dataset**. It must include:

- authoritative source citation
- retrieval date
- raw-to-clean script
- five clearly named variables
- documented transformations
- one visualization
- README with exact reproduction instructions
- a short note on licensing / redistribution status

Do not upload restricted or confidential data.

## What happens next

Applications are scored against `programs/modern/SELECTION_RUBRIC.md`.

Shortlisted applicants may receive one scoped follow-up question or reproducibility check. Acceptance into the cohort does **not** count as a public contribution. Contributor status is counted only after verified work is merged or released.

## Research pods

- Labor Markets
- Firms + AI
- Trade + Industrial Policy
- Water + Climate
- Public Finance
- Financial Markets
- Narratives + Institutions

## Fellowship standard

Fellows must complete the requirements in `programs/modern/FELLOWSHIP.md`; attendance alone is insufficient.

## Privacy

Applicant records and review decisions are kept private. Only aggregate application statistics may be published after the cycle closes. Do not send passport numbers, national IDs, medical information, or other unnecessary sensitive data.
