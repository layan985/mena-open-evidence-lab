Counting rule

An attributable external-use record requires a named Lab output, attributable user or institution, concrete use, date and a reviewable evidence trail. Private or undisclosed use may be reported as an aggregate count when known, but it is not converted into a public validation, citation, case study or institutional endorsement.

What would upgrade the record

With permission, each independent user can become a public record containing the output used, use category, date, organization or researcher name, evidence link or correspondence reference, and whether the use was analysis, teaching, operational research, policy work or reproduction. Until then, the aggregate remains deliberately separate.
