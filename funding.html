Funding & independence

Money does not get to rewrite evidence state.

This register records disclosable funding and commissioning relationships with purpose, period, research-control terms, publication rights, conflict status and disclosure level. An empty register means no qualifying public record is being claimed.

Required fields

funder_or_client_class · amount_or_band · currency · purpose · period · research_control · publication_rights · conflict_status · public_disclosure_level

Independence rule

Commissioned work may constrain disclosure because of confidentiality, but it does not change the evidence labels, validation threshold, correction rules or the distinction between public research and client-specific findings.
