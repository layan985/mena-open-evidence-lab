What this table is for

It is designed for researchers assembling historical MENA panels, theses, policy reports, dashboards and forecasting datasets. The object is not to declare a series “good” or “bad.” It is to prevent a methodological break from being mistaken for an economic event.

What it is not

This pilot is not exhaustive. It contains ten source-verified records selected because the producer documentation is strong enough to support a concrete user action. Generalization about the prevalence of statistical breaks in the region is withheld until the registry is larger and independently reviewed.
