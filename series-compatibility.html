MENA Series Compatibility Table

A practical answer to one question: when a statistical series changes base, method, survey design, classification or historical treatment, what can still be compared directly—and what should the researcher use instead?

Interpretation rule. A bridge can be official, partial/provisional, or not yet verified. “Comparable” never means the measurement system is identical. It means the producer supplies enough of a bridge for the stated use, or the user action explicitly tells the researcher how to preserve the break.
