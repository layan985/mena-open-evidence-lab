Independent research infrastructure · MENA

Measure what was knowable, not only what history looks like now.

The Lab builds source-linked economic evidence with the time dimension left intact: release dates, vintages, revisions, definitions, methods and uncertainty. The current flagship is MENA-RTD, a real-time data and vintages system for reconstructing the information that actually existed at historical dates.

New flagship · MENA-RTD · seed 0.1

The economy exists before the statistic does.

Q1 2026 ended on 31 March. In the current sourced seed, Saudi Arabia's flash GDP estimate appears 29 days after quarter-end; its fuller release arrives after 69 days; Morocco's Q1 national accounts after 90; Jordan's observed English GDP page after 96; and Oman's formal quarterly-national-accounts catalogue publication after 103.

The important result is not just speed. Release architecture differs. MENA-RTD preserves flash, full, catalogue, observed-public and revision clocks separately instead of forcing them into one date.

New measurement objects

The goal is reusable research infrastructure, not another generic regional dashboard.

Regional information completeness

What fraction of the regional economy was statistically observable on date d?

A weight-explicit completeness curve under equal-country, GDP or population weights. Release clock and weight vintage are part of the statistic.

Information half-life

T50 · T75 · T90

Days after a reference period until 50%, 75% or 90% of the chosen regional weight has released the defined statistic.

Revision risk

How uncertain was the first estimate in practice?

Empirical first-to-later revision distributions, news-versus-noise tests and ranking uncertainty without pretending revision risk is a sampling confidence interval.

Latent economic state

Official data + physical signals

A pseudo-real-time state-space research programme designed to combine official releases with validated high-frequency signals while preserving posterior uncertainty.

Open firm panel

Observable evidence, probabilistic state

Firm-month evidence with an explicit active/dormant/exited latent state rather than declaring a firm dead because a source went quiet.

State capital graph

Who funds, owns, awards and targets whom?

A time-stamped network foundation for causal research on procurement, SOEs, sovereign funds and industrial policy.

Technical standard

Try to kill every finding before publishing it.

Frozen raw inputs, hashes, versioned transformations, codebooks, provenance, missingness analysis, sensitivity tests, uncertainty, out-of-sample validation where relevant, negative results and replication packages are part of the output. Every material conclusion must survive at least one deliberately hostile but defensible specification.
