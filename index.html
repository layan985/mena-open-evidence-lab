Latest discovery · Measurement Anomaly 001 · Saudi Arabia

Saudi Arabia’s 2023 economy went from shrinking to growing without 2023 changing.

An earlier official vintage recorded 2023 real GDP growth at −0.8%. After GASTAT’s comprehensive national-accounts revision, the same year is recorded at +0.5%. The 1.3-point revision crosses zero, changing the qualitative description of the year from contraction to growth.

The economy changes. So does the machinery used to measure it. This is a statistical-vintage event, not a claim that underlying 2023 activity changed retroactively.

Evidence archive · Jordan · Evidence Brief

Jordan's annual headline CPI accelerated through May, then eased slightly in June.

The official year-on-year headline rate rose from 1.06% in January to 2.83% in May 2026, before easing to 2.79% in June. The month-on-month rate cooled to 0.14% by June.

This is a descriptive source-linked result. It does not identify component contributions, household welfare effects or a causal mechanism.
