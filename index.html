Independent measurement economics infrastructure · MENA

The economy changes. So does the machinery used to measure it.

MENA Evidence Lab studies economic measurement itself: when information became knowable, how official values changed later, when definitions and methods moved, whether calendar effects remain, and which conclusions survive a revised statistical system.

Why preserve statistical history?

Saudi Arabia’s measured 2023 GDP rose by SAR 566 billion.

GASTAT’s comprehensive revision raised measured 2023 nominal GDP by 14.1% and changed the measured structure of the economy. Nothing here implies SAR 566 billion of output appeared overnight. The measurement system improved—and economic history changed with it.

This is the Lab’s object of study: not whether revisions are “bad,” but how much an empirical conclusion depends on the statistical vintage and methodology used to produce it.
