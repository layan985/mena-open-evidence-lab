Latest evidence · Jordan · Evidence Brief

Jordan's annual headline CPI accelerated through May, then eased slightly in June.

The official year-on-year headline rate rose from 1.06% in January to 2.83% in May 2026, before easing to 2.79% in June. The month-on-month rate cooled to 0.14% by June.

This is a descriptive source-linked result. It does not identify component contributions, household welfare effects or a causal mechanism.
