ADVISORY / COMMISSIONED

Institutional Evidence Sprints

Commissioned work is separated from public research by visible rules for funding, publication rights, review rights, suppression and conflicts.

ENGAGEMENT MODELINDEPENDENCE
Publication standard: released objects expose status, evidence type, methods, sources, QA, limitations, code/data where available, corrections and review state. Unavailable evidence is recorded as pending rather than implied.
Independent challenge

Outside review is a separate workstream.

The Lab does not convert founder reruns, informal feedback or collaboration discussions into external validation. Review and contribution records are created only when attributable work exists.

REPRODUCE

Run a release independently

Rebuild an analysis or dataset from a clean environment and submit a reviewable reproduction record.

AUDIT

Challenge a source or method

Check source capture, coding, transformations, comparability, tests or a material methodological decision.

CONTRIBUTE

Own a bounded research task

Contributions are credited by documented work. Open advisory roles are listed separately and are never implied before acceptance.
