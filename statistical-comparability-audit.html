Statistical Comparability Audit

Give the Lab a report, dashboard, M&E framework, market assessment or cross-country analysis. We audit whether the numbers being compared are actually the same statistical objects.

Request an audit →

Every material statistic is checked against nine failure modes.

The public Comparability Graph shows the method before a client buys it.

01
Source & vintage

Which exact release supplied the number, and was a later revision substituted into an earlier information set?

02
Population & denominator

Who is actually inside the statistic? Nationality, age universe, resident population, labour force, firms or households.

03
Concept

Do similarly named measures answer the same question, or has unemployment quietly become broader labour underutilization?

04
Method regime

Survey redesigns, administrative-data adoption, rebasing, classifications and estimator changes.

05
Time comparability

Does the series survive methodology changes and historical breaks?

06
Cross-country comparability

Are national definitions actually aligned closely enough for the claim being made?

07
Transformation integrity

Growth rates, rebasing, deflation, currency conversion, annualization and aggregation are reconstructed.

08
Citation integrity

Every important claim is bound back to the release that supports it.

09
Claim boundary

Where evidence does not support a clean comparison, the deliverable says so instead of forcing one.

Deliverable

Green / amber / red evidence ledger + corrected data layer.

Each material statistic is classified with the exact reason, primary-source evidence and corrected replacement where possible. The client receives the audit ledger, corrected dataset, source register, revision notes and replacement wording for claims that fail.

Typical use cases: multi-country reports, donor evaluations, market-entry studies, policy dashboards, research papers and investor/risk analysis.

Scope

Price is quoted to the number of claims, countries, indicators, historical depth and source-reconstruction burden. A short diagnostic can be bounded to the highest-risk claims before a full audit.

Research independence

Commissioned findings do not change the labels or validation standards used on public Lab research.
