Review & Contribute

Make outside scrutiny attributable and reproducible.

The highest-value contributions are bounded, inspectable pieces of work: reproduce a release, audit a source trail, independently code a sample, challenge a method or own a documented research task. Review, contribution and advisory appointment are separate records.

Independent reproduction

Rebuild a release

Run or reconstruct the relevant analysis from a clean environment and provide a reviewable record of the result, environment and discrepancies.

Source audit

Check provenance

Verify source documents, extraction, transformations, dates, vintages, comparability and saved source hashes against a published release.

Second coding

Code independently

Apply the published coding rules to a bounded sample without seeing the founder-coded result, then compare disagreements transparently.

Methods review

Challenge an inference

Review estimands, assumptions, uncertainty, robustness choices, failure modes and what evidence would materially weaken a claim.

Contributor task

Own a defined unit of work

Contribute data collection, tests, documentation, reproducibility infrastructure or a methods note with a public artifact and contribution record.

Advisory roles

Three appointments remain open

Methods & Reproducibility, Regional Data, and Open Research & Governance. No person is listed until acceptance and a public appointment record exists.

How work is counted

Founder reruns are internal QA. Informal comments are feedback. Independent reproduction requires outside execution or reconstruction with reviewable evidence. External use requires an attributable user or institution, a concrete use, a date and an evidence trail.

Recognition

Accepted contributions record the contributor, contribution type, affected artifact and date. Authorship follows documented intellectual contribution; review or advisory activity alone is not authorship.

Start with a bounded task

Browse open repository issues, the contribution-task register, the reviewer packet, and the current validation states. For substantial work, open an issue before beginning so the scope and evidence standard are explicit.

Review, contribution and advisory appointment are counted separately.
