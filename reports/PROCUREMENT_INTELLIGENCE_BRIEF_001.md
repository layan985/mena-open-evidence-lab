## Executive view

The current MENA procurement snapshot contains **32 open opportunities**, **38 pipeline notices**, **140 recent procurement plans**, and **11,799 normalized MENA contract-award records** from official World Bank sources.

This is not a list of scraped headlines. The product normalizes opportunity, buyer, project, reference, notice type, category, procurement method, publication date, deadline and source links while preserving source boundaries and limitations.

## 2. Why this matters commercially

A raw procurement portal forces a user to search notice by notice. The intelligence layer is designed to answer different questions:

- Which buyers repeatedly commission data, M&E, software, research or advisory work?
- Which opportunities are near-term versus pipeline?
- Which procurement methods dominate a target segment?
- Which historical awards provide benchmark ranges or incumbent signals?
- Which countries and project families are repeatedly active?
- Which opportunities fit a specific evidence, data or analytics capability?

## 3. Example fit signals for an evidence/data provider

The current open notices already contain several capability-adjacent patterns:

- data analysis and reporting;
- monitoring and evaluation;
- software development and maintenance;
- digital transformation;
- consultancy design and supervision;
- specialist individual-consultant roles.

The point is not that the Lab should bid on every notice. The commercial value is qualification: identify a narrow subset where the buyer, scope, method, deadline and past procurement behavior justify action.

## 4. Evidence architecture

The current product draws from three official World Bank source classes:

1. current procurement opportunities;
2. disclosed procurement plans / potential opportunities;
3. IPF contract awards since FY2020.

The award corpus is normalized without inventing undisclosed award values.

## 5. Known limitations

- Open opportunities are a point-in-time snapshot.
- Award amounts are committed, not disbursed.
- Supplier country is place of registration and may not equal delivery location or ultimate origin.
- Awards may exclude subcontractors and cofinancing.
- Joint-venture values may be split across members.
- This edition structurally normalizes World Bank sources only; UNGM, EU and GIZ should be added as separately attributable source layers rather than blended into the existing counts.
