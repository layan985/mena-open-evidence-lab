# Methods paper outline

## Working title
**The MENA Economic Narrative and Market Stress Observatory: A Reproducible Framework for Bilingual Economic Measurement**

## Sections
1. Motivation: fragmented MENA measurement across languages, institutions and conflict contexts.
2. Data architecture: source hierarchy, observation schema, versioning.
3. Provenance and audit: traceability, verification, missingness, corrections.
4. Four measurement layers: macro, market, policy, narrative.
5. Bilingual methodology: annotation, translation risk, semantic drift, model evaluation.
6. Composite-score publication gate: why aggregation is prohibited before coverage/audit thresholds.
7. Measurement pathologies: conflict-period base effects, structural breaks, stale releases, incomparable definitions, publication latency.
8. Validation: independent audits, sensitivity analyses, benchmark construction.
9. Limitations.
10. Reproducibility: code, data, DOI, manifests, release history.
