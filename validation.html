Evidence & validation

Trust is release-specific.

Internal QA, independent verification, independent reproduction and external use answer different questions. The Lab records them separately so a user, client or collaborator never becomes a reviewer by implication.

01 / Internal QA

Producer-owned

Tests, reruns, source checks and documentation performed within the production process.

02 / Independent verification

Different person

Source audit, blinded second coding or methods challenge by someone other than the primary producer.

03 / Reproduction

Outside execution

Clean-environment reconstruction with a reviewable result, deviations and discrepancy record.

04 / External use

Actual use

A research object is used outside the production team. Use alone does not certify correctness.

Research reuse and citations

Reported independent use is visible in the external-use ledger. Public citations, external contributions, independent reproductions and methods reviews remain zero until attributable evidence exists.

Counting rule

A founder rerun, internal CI, a collaborator discussion, a user, or an informal compliment cannot receive an independent-validation designation. The relevant outside task must be completed and recorded.
