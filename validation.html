Independent review is an institutional asset, not a decorative badge.

Release-specific versus institution-level evidence

The Lab may report aggregate completed reviews and reproductions where confidentiality or publication rights prevent full public attribution. That aggregate does not permit a specific dataset or paper to carry an independently reproduced badge unless the corresponding record is attached to that release.

Correction standard

The current unresolved-correction count is zero. Corrections are governed by the correction policy and machine-readable correction register.
