Research Governance

No research object validates itself.

The Lab now separates production, verification and release authority. A founder-produced release may be published as a release candidate after internal QA, but it cannot receive an independent-review or reproduction designation unless a different identifiable person performs the relevant verification and leaves a reviewable record.

01 / Produce

Primary producer

Collects, codes, transforms or builds the research object. The producer is named on the release record.

02 / Verify

Independent verifier

Checks a source trail, performs blinded second coding, or reconstructs the object. For an external-validation claim, this person must be different from the primary producer.

03 / Challenge

Methods reviewer

Where the output makes inferential claims, a reviewer challenges assumptions, estimands, uncertainty, robustness and material failure modes.

04 / Release

Release approver

Approves publication state and corrections. Approval cannot substitute for independent verification; the evidence state stays pending until the relevant outside record exists.

Role-separation rule. The same person may produce and approve an early release candidate, but the same person cannot be counted as both primary producer and independent verifier for that release. Founder reruns remain internal QA.

Release gates

Draft
Producer named; work may still change materially.
No outside verification required.
Release candidate
Internal QA, provenance and limitations documented.
May still be founder-produced and founder-approved.
Independently reviewed
Different identifiable person completes the defined source-audit, second-coding or methods-review task.
External verification record required.
Reproduced
Outside party reconstructs the relevant analysis or data object from a clean environment and reports discrepancies.
Independent reproduction record required.
