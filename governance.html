Research Governance

Independence where independence matters.

The Lab is founder-led today. This page makes that concentration explicit and defines the external advisory layer being built around methods, regional data interpretation and open-research governance.

Research integrity, conflicts, corrections, openness and data governance.

Decision rights

Major methodological changes, retractions, conflict decisions and material changes to research-independence policy are documented. External advisors are advisory unless a future charter explicitly delegates approval rights.

Appointments

No person is presented as an advisor until they have accepted the role and a public appointment record exists. Names are not inferred from conversations or informal feedback.

Conflicts

Material personal, financial or commissioning conflicts are disclosed alongside affected work. Commissioned work follows the funding and publication-rights disclosure standard.

Records

Once the advisory layer is active, the Lab will publish an advisory charter, conflicts register and annual governance statement, with meeting notes where confidentiality permits.
