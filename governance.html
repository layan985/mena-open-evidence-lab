Decision rights

Major methodological changes, retractions, conflict decisions and material changes to research-independence policy are documented. Advisory roles do not carry approval authority unless a published charter explicitly delegates it.

Appointments

An advisor is listed only after accepting the role and a public appointment record is created. Informal advice, email feedback, collaboration discussions and one-off review do not constitute appointment.

Conflicts

Material personal, financial or commissioning conflicts are disclosed alongside affected work. Commissioned work follows the funding, review-rights and publication-rights disclosure standard.

Governance records

The Lab publishes the External Advisory Charter, Funding & Independence standard, institutional policies and annual governance reporting.

Open review routes

Independent reviewers and bounded contributors can use the Review & Contribute page. Review work is recorded separately from advisory appointment.
