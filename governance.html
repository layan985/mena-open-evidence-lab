Decision rights

Major methodological changes, retractions, conflict decisions and material changes to research-independence policy are documented. Advisory roles do not carry approval authority unless a published charter explicitly delegates it.

Appointments

An advisor is listed only after accepting the role and a public appointment record is created.

Conflicts

Material personal, financial or commissioning conflicts are disclosed alongside affected work. Commissioned work follows the funding, review-rights and publication-rights disclosure standard.

Governance records

The Lab publishes the advisory charter, funding and independence standard, institutional policies and annual governance reporting.
