# Release policy

## v0.x
Pilot and pre-audit releases. Suitable for methodological development, not for claims of validated composite measurement.

## v1.0 gate
Requires documented coverage thresholds, provenance completeness, an independent sample audit, versioned methodology, a machine-readable data dictionary, a correction policy, and an archived release with DOI.

## Composite-score gate
No composite MODE score is published until macro, market, policy, and bilingual narrative layers pass their predeclared coverage and audit thresholds.
