## Private engagement templates

Private operational records must remain access-controlled and must not be committed with client data. The required template set is:

`Proposal` · `Statement of Work` · `NDA` · `Data Request` · `Inception Note` · `Data Contract` · `QA Register` · `Claim Ledger` · `Decision Memo` · `Client Acceptance` · `Publication Rights Matrix`.

Contract, NDA and data-protection language requires appropriate legal review before commercial reliance.

## Canonical registries

Public machine-readable state belongs under `data/`. Existing canonical registers include engagements, external use, corrections, people, funding, releases/manifest and methods-series records. Site claims should resolve to these registers wherever a structured record exists.

## Export rule

A PDF or DOCX is a presentation artifact. The repository source and machine-readable register remain the canonical operational record unless an engagement contract explicitly defines otherwise.
