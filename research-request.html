Public research requests

Suggest an indicator, dataset, replication or evidence question.

Requests are inputs to the research agenda, not promises of acceptance. Strong requests identify a real evidence gap, affected users and the sources or disagreements that make the question difficult.

The form prepares an email to the monitored research mailbox. Sensitive or restricted material should not be submitted through this page.

Requests are screened for relevance, feasibility, evidence access and independence.
