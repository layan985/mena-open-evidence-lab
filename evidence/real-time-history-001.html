The research question

How different is the economic history available to decision-makers in real time from the history researchers download later?

That question cannot be answered from three observations. RTH-001 is infrastructure, not an estimate. The Lab is separating three things that are often collapsed: a first official vintage, a later directly comparable vintage, and a methodological break that should not be treated as an ordinary numerical revision.

Precommitted measurement rule

revision_pp = later_comparable_value − first_value

If the statistical object changes materially — for example through rebasing, classification, scope, reconstruction or another methodology change — the comparison is marked BREAK instead of being folded into a revision statistic.

What this page does not claim

It does not claim that MENA data are unusually unreliable. It does not estimate a regional average revision. It does not imply that either LIVE record will change. A future comparable value may be higher, lower or identical.

Sources and data

The Saudi Q1 2025 figures are reconstructed from archived official GASTAT flash and quarterly GDP releases. The two LIVE records are maintained in the MENA Statistical Vintage Register.
