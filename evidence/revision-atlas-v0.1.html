METHOD REVIEW · excluded

Bahrain · 2024

A May 2025 preliminary release reported 2.6% annual real GDP growth. The current official constant-price series observed on 20 August 2026 reports 2.901%.

Numerical difference: +0.301 pp.

The national-accounts database and classification framework changed. Until that relationship is adjudicated, this is not labelled an ordinary revision.

The rule that matters

Changed number ≠ revision by default.

A numerical revision is computed only when the earlier and later values describe the same statistical object closely enough for the comparison to be meaningful. Rebasing, classification, scope or reconstruction changes are kept separate.

What v0.1 establishes

Official economic history has vintages. Those vintages can change, can remain unchanged, or can become non-comparable because the statistical framework itself changes. The Atlas is designed to preserve all three outcomes rather than backfilling a single clean-looking historical series.

What v0.1 does not establish

It does not show that MENA statistics are unusually unreliable. It does not estimate a MENA-wide revision distribution. It does not interpret revisions as errors. It does not infer the cause of a changed estimate from its magnitude or direction.
