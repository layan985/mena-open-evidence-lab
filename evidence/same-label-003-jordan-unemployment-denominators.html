Same Label, Different Statistic · 003 · 22 Aug 2026

Jordan's Q1 unemployment rate was 16.1%. For Jordanians, it was 21.1%.

Both are official Q1 2026 labour-market statistics. The difference begins with the population being measured.

Total population16.1%Jordanians and non-Jordanians
Jordanians21.1%Jordanian population aged 15+
Claim boundary. This is not a contradiction and not evidence that one estimate is wrong. It is a denominator problem: the headline changes when the population universe changes.

Why the label is insufficient

Writing only “Jordan unemployment: 16.1%” strips away information needed to compare it with a nationality-bounded statistic elsewhere. A valid comparison must carry its population universe with the value.

The graph consequence

The Lab records the edge between these Q1 2026 figures as different_population. It refuses a direct comparison until the research question specifies which population is relevant.
