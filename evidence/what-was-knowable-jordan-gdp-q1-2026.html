Why this record matters

Prospective vintages turn later revisions into measurable evidence instead of anecdotes discovered after the fact.

If Jordan later republishes Q1 2026 under a directly comparable statistical concept, MENA Lab will append the new value and calculate the difference. If the underlying statistical object changes materially, the comparison will be marked BREAK rather than forced into an ordinary revision statistic.

Precommitted comparison rule

revision_pp = later_comparable_Q1_2026_growth − 2.9

The comparison applies only to a later directly comparable Q1 2026 constant-price GDP growth estimate. Base-year, scope, classification, price/volume or other material methodology changes are evaluated before any revision magnitude is reported.

Claim boundary

Claim: Jordan's Department of Statistics reports Q1 2026 GDP growth at constant prices of 2.9% year on year. The release states that preliminary sectoral estimates show positive growth across all activities, including manufacturing +5.3%, agriculture +6.8%, mining and quarrying +4.7%, electricity and gas +4.3%, and wholesale/retail +3.2%.

Not claimed: that the headline 2.9% figure itself is formally labelled “preliminary” in the same wording as every sectoral estimate; that a later estimate must differ; or that any later difference necessarily reflects an error.

Contribution structure

The release attributes the largest contribution to headline growth to manufacturing at 0.86 percentage points, followed by agriculture at 0.40 pp and wholesale/retail at 0.27 pp. Mining and quarrying contribute 0.18 pp and electricity/gas 0.09 pp.

Source and provenance

Publisher: Department of Statistics, Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan. Official release date: 5 July 2026. Official source file: GDP_Q1_2026_en.pdf.

The Lab verified the official PDF content through the public source but did not successfully retrieve the binary bytes through its separate hashing path during this freeze. No SHA-256 is therefore published.
