Method Break 003 · Tunisia · Prices

A missing publication period can be part of the methodology story.

Tunisia's quarterly residential real-estate price index was interrupted during 2022–2023 while INS revised its calculation procedures, then resumed publication with revised historical values.

What changed

Institut National de la Statistique states that publication of the quarterly real-estate price index was interrupted during 2022 and 2023 while calculation procedures were revised. Publication resumed with the first-quarter 2024 release.

Why this matters

A researcher downloading the currently available series sees a continuous numerical history, but the publication history was not continuous. The current 2022–2023 values belong to the revised method regime and should be treated as revised historical estimates rather than silently assumed to be the exact information set available during those quarters.

User rule

For current trend analysis, use the revised INS series. For real-time reproduction of analysis made during 2022–2023, preserve or recover the contemporaneous publication state instead of substituting today's revised history.

Source trail

Primary producer: Institut National de la Statistique (Tunisia).

  • Q1 2024 release
  • Technical sheet

Claim boundary

This note does not infer that the revision was unusually large, problematic, or evidence of low statistical quality. It records the change in method and publication history so users can distinguish current revised history from contemporaneous information.
