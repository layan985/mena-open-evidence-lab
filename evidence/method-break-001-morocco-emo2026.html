Method Break 001 · Morocco · Labour market · 2026

Do not splice Morocco's 2026 unemployment series onto the old one.

HCP's EMO2026 is not simply a larger version of the previous labour survey. The statistical object changed.

Finding

For historical comparison with 2026, use HCP's EMO-compatible backcast indicators for 2017–2025 where available — not the previously published ENE series.

What changed

HCP replaced the former ENE labour-force survey with EMO2026. The redesign changes the concept of employment, introduces strict unemployment, expands the measurement of labour underutilization, updates the sampling frame using the 2024 population and housing census, increases the annual sample from 90,000 to 135,000 households and introduces a new rotation design.

The comparability consequence

HCP explicitly states that EMO2026 results are not directly comparable with the previously published ENE results because of conceptual and methodological breaks. HCP is therefore publishing provisional reconstructed indicators for 2017–2025 under the new concepts.

Research-use rule: a chart that places a 2026 EMO unemployment estimate directly after an old ENE estimate without marking the break risks treating a method change as an economic change. Use the backcast series where possible; otherwise mark the break and withhold a continuous-trend claim.

Why this matters

When a survey changes, the most convenient historical series is not necessarily the valid comparison series. The method version is part of the observation.

Claim boundary

This note does not assess whether EMO2026 is better or worse than the prior survey. The producer's documentation indicates that the new design changes statistical concepts and comparability. The note records the consequence for downstream research.
