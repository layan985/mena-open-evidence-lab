Impact evidence standard

Every non-zero claim should resolve to a record with date, output, external actor, use category and evidence. Where confidentiality prevents public attribution, an aggregate may be reported separately with its disclosure boundary made explicit.

What does not count

Website traffic, social impressions, prospective use, compliments, invitations to talk, applications, or organizations merely appearing in source data do not become “impact” by themselves.
