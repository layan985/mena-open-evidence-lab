Measurement Anomaly 001 · Saudi Arabia · 2023 GDP

Saudi Arabia’s reported 2023 growth changed sign.

Pre-revision vintage−0.8%2023 real GDP growth
Comprehensive revision+0.5%2023 real GDP growth

The final pre-comprehensive-revision 2024/Q4 publication still showed 2023 real GDP growth at −0.8%. The revised annual national accounts report +0.5%. The vintage transition therefore changes the qualitative description of 2023 from contraction to growth: a +1.3 percentage-point narrative reversal.

This is a statistical-measurement event, not a claim that the underlying economy changed after 2023 ended.

Research object: narrative reversal

A narrative reversal occurs when a later official statistical vintage changes the qualitative conclusion implied by an earlier comparable vintage.

Sign reversal

Contraction → growth

Saudi Arabia 2023 is the first published Vault case.

Ranking reversal

A > B → B > A

Cross-country or sector ordering changes under later comparable vintages.

Threshold reversal

Below → above

Derived ratios or growth thresholds cross a decision-relevant boundary after revision.

Build path

The Saudi case is the seed, not the finished regional archive.

  1. Reconstruct annual and quarterly Saudi GDP vintage chains for 2011–2023.
  2. Detect sign, ranking, acceleration/deceleration and threshold reversals.
  3. Replicate the same structure for Jordan, Morocco and Tunisia using documented methodology-shock episodes.
  4. Publish revision-risk distributions only after sufficiently dense comparable vintage chains exist.

Current state: first verified narrative-reversal pair live · regional historical reconstruction in build · no unobserved vintage magnitudes are inferred.
