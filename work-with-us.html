Engagement standard

Every paid engagement begins with a written scope covering the decision question, evidence perimeter, source access, deliverables, timeline, price and payment terms, confidentiality, publication rights, draft-review rights, suppression rights and conflict disclosures. The operating workflow is documented in the Commissioned Work Standard.

Public record standard

Where publication rights permit, the Lab may publish a restrained Engagement Record containing the decision question, evidence architecture, public method note, selected redacted figures, deliverables manifest, public-source perimeter, limitations and disclosure boundary. Client-specific data, conclusions, restricted appendices and sensitive recommendations remain confidential unless explicit rights permit release.

Institutional contact

The Lab’s domain-based research, partnerships, data and corrections inboxes are reserved as the institutional contact layer. Public email links will be activated only when mail routing is confirmed, so the website does not advertise an unverified delivery channel.

Research independence

Commissioned analysis does not change the evidence labels used on public research. A client may review commissioned work within the agreed scope, but review rights, publication rights and any suppression rights are disclosed. See Funding & Independence.
