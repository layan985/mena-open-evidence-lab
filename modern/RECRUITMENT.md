# MODERN Cohort 01 Recruitment

**Program:** MODERN — MENA Open Data, Evidence & Reproducibility Network  
**Host:** MENA Open Data & Evidence Lab  
**Cohort:** August 2026–February 8, 2027  
**Target cohort:** 23 contributors across 7 universities, including 7 Pod Leads and 11 Undergraduate Research Fellows.

## What MODERN is

MODERN is a production network for undergraduate and early-career researchers who want to build public, reproducible MENA economic and social-data infrastructure. Members do not receive credit for attendance alone. Every credited contributor must make a documented contribution to a public research object.

## Open roles

### 7 Pod Leads
1. Labor Markets
2. Firms + AI
3. Trade + Industrial Policy
4. Water + Climate
5. Public Finance
6. Financial Markets
7. Narratives + Institutions

Pod Leads coordinate 2 dataset releases, review pull requests, enforce the release checklist, run weekly pod check-ins, and keep provenance and contributor records current.

### Research Contributors
Contributors collect, clean, standardize, validate, document, review, or reproduce data releases. Typical workload: 3–5 hours/week during active production periods.

### 11 Undergraduate Research Fellows
Fellows are selected from the contributor cohort. Fellowship completion requires public technical contributions, workshop participation, a reproducibility audit, and an individual capstone contribution.

## Eligibility

Students and recent graduates from economics, statistics, data science, computer science, public policy, finance, business, political science, sociology, media/communication, environmental studies, and adjacent fields are welcome. Applicants do not need to be advanced programmers, but they must be willing to use Git/GitHub and work transparently.

## Selection evidence

Applicants submit:
- university, degree, year, country, timezone;
- GitHub profile if available;
- preferred pod(s);
- software used: Python, R, Stata, SQL/DuckDB, Git, spreadsheets;
- one example of research/data work if available;
- a short screening task;
- weekly availability through February 8, 2027.

## Screening task

Choose one public MENA-related dataset and submit a GitHub repository containing:
1. the raw-source citation and access date;
2. a script or notebook that cleans at least 5 variables;
3. at least 3 documented transformations;
4. duplicate and missing-value checks;
5. one useful visualization or summary table;
6. a short README explaining what changed and why.

The task is evaluated on reproducibility, documentation, judgment, code clarity, and research integrity—not polish alone.

## Selection rubric

- Reproducibility and organization — 25%
- Data-cleaning judgment — 20%
- Documentation/provenance — 20%
- Technical execution — 15%
- Reliability/availability — 10%
- Research fit and curiosity — 10%

## Evidence-first credit policy

A person is counted as a MODERN contributor only after a traceable contribution exists: accepted pull request, validated dataset work, workshop production, documented review, or another approved public deliverable. Being listed in a chat, spreadsheet, or application form is not sufficient.
