Why the edges matter

The graph stores the relationship between measures, not just the measures themselves.

Jordan 16.1 ↔ Jordan 21.1 · different_population · directly comparable: no

Same country and quarter, but one statistic covers the total resident population while the other covers Jordanians aged 15+.

Morocco 10.8 ↔ Morocco 22.5 · different_concept · directly comparable: no

The broader measure intentionally captures unmet labour demand that strict unemployment excludes.

Saudi 6.4 ↔ Jordan 16.1 · different_population · directly comparable: no

One is nationality-bounded; the other is not.

Saudi 6.4 ↔ Jordan 21.1 · requires_method_review

Both are nationality-bounded unemployment statistics, but the Lab withholds a direct comparison until the age universe, survey design and labour-force definitions are aligned field by field.

Research claim

The same label is not sufficient evidence of comparability. Population universe, concept, survey design, method regime, release vintage and historical breaks must be part of the observation.
