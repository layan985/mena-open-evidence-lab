Object 02 · What Was Knowable?

Jordan · 18 June 2026 monetary-policy decision.

Do not explain a June decision with July data.

The Central Bank of Jordan maintained its key interest rate at 5.75% on 18 June 2026. The reconstruction below freezes the information set at the decision date.

17 JUN 2026

May CPI available in Arabic

DoS reported 1.88% cumulative inflation for January–May 2026 and 2.83% year-on-year inflation for May.

18 JUN 2026

Policy decision

CBJ maintained the key rate at 5.75%. Its release cited reserves of USD 27.2bn at end-May, 9.5 months of import coverage, 1.88% five-month inflation, 13.3% remittance growth, 1.6% national-export growth and a 9.2% decline in tourism income.

AFTER 18 JUN 2026

Post-decision data excluded

June CPI and later macro releases do not belong in the reconstructed decision-time information set.

What Release 002 establishes

Comparability requires metadata

The same CPI label can conceal different reference years, weight systems, geographic populations and index architectures.

Historical analysis requires vintages

A policy evaluation is invalid if it silently imports information published after the decision date.

Transparency is measurable

Methodology, release timing, machine readability, archive quality and revision visibility can be audited as separate evidence dimensions.

Research standard

Release 002 records verified institutional evidence as verified, unresolved fields as partial or unverified, and never infers a negative from a failed search. Scores are pilot evidence-coverage summaries, not claims about institutional quality beyond the audited dimensions.
