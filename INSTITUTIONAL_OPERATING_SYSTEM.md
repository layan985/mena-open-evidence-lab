# Institutional Operating System

_Last updated: 16 August 2026_

## Governance stack

The Lab maintains operational rules for research governance, independence, funding disclosure, conflicts, corrections/retractions, data/code availability, reproducibility, research ethics, privacy, information security, retention/deletion, authorship, contribution, external review, commissioned work and accessibility.

Policies are not decorative web pages. Each policy must identify the decision owner, triggering condition, required record and correction/escalation path.

## Team architecture

Initial operating roles:

- Research Director — core;
- Research Engineer / Data Engineer — fractional to core;
- Research Analyst — project/fractional;
- Data / Research Assistants — project-based;
- Methods reviewers — external;
- Regional source reviewers — external;
- Research information designer — fractional;
- Partnerships / operations — fractional initially.

Headcount grows after durable revenue and work volume justify it.

## Contributor program

Contributors complete bounded research objects: source reconstruction, verification, replication, country source maps, second coding, metadata improvement, documentation or QA tests. Public credit follows accepted work and a reviewable contribution record.

## Advisory layer

Three active advisory roles are sufficient: Methods & Reproducibility, Regional Data, and Governance & Open Research. Advisors challenge institutional decisions; they do not automatically approve every publication.

## Client workflow

Lead → qualification → conflict check → discovery → proposal → contracting → confidentiality/data terms → data receipt → analysis → QA → red team → delivery → acceptance → invoice → closeout → publication permissions → follow-up.

## Private evidence room

Each commissioned engagement maintains an access-controlled evidence room with the Executive Decision Memo, Evidence Report, deliverables, source register, QA register, limitations, claim ledger, methodology, technical appendix, reproduction package, meeting decisions, change requests and final acceptance materials.

## Case-study rule

A confidential case study may disclose only the industry/problem/method/data perimeter/deliverable/result class permitted by the client. Client names and production results are not published without explicit rights.

## Vanity-metric rule

Page views, likes, followers and stars are not primary institutional KPIs. Primary evidence of maturity is released datasets, independent reviews, reproductions, external users, citations, paying institutions, repeat clients, corrections, source-hash coverage and reproducible outputs.

## High-end boutique threshold

The 12-month operating target is approximately: 3–4 flagship released data products; 8–12 serious publications; 4+ methods/replication releases; 5+ external reviewers; 2+ independent reproductions; 5+ external contributors; 5+ paying institutions; 2+ repeat clients; 1–2 Evidence Desk retainers; 3 named advisors; 1+ institutional partner; 3+ workshops; 5+ research/data external uses; a live correction system; flagship DOIs; and an annual transparency report.

These are targets, not current claims. Current values remain governed by their evidence registries.
