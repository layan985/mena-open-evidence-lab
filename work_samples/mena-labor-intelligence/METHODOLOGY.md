# Methodology

The work sample extracts eight selected MENA economies for 2020 and 2025 from the World Bank HCI+ panel.

Primary fields:
- working-age labor-force participation;
- working-age employment;
- youth participation/employment;
- wage-employment share;
- HCI+ total and on-the-job learning component.

Upstream rate fields are stored on a 0–1 scale. Display metrics convert them to percentages. Gender gaps are male minus female rates in percentage points.

Quality gates:
1. unique country-year;
2. rate fields constrained to [0,1];
3. source URL and access date retained;
4. no silent imputation.

All comparisons are descriptive.
