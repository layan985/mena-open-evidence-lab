# Data dictionary

`lfp_wa_mf/m/f` — working-age labor-force participation, total/male/female.

`emp_wa_mf/m/f` — working-age employment rate.

`lfp_ya_mf/m/f` — youth labor-force participation.

`emp_ya_mf/m/f` — youth employment rate.

`shr_wemp_wa_mf/m/f` — share in wage employment.

`hcip_mf/m/f` — HCI+ total score.

`hcip_work_component_*` — on-the-job learning component.

`*_gender_gap_pp` — male minus female in percentage points.

`source_url` / `source_accessed` — provenance fields.
