Institutional Policies

The boring pages are part of the research system.

These policies operationalize the existing transparency standard. They are concise now and should become more detailed as the Lab accumulates staff, contributors, users and commissioned work.

Research Integrity

No evidence label exceeds the strongest documented validation state. Material errors trigger correction or retraction records.

Research Ethics

Human-subject or sensitive-data work requires an explicit ethical and legal basis before collection.

Conflicts of Interest

Material financial, personal and commissioning conflicts are disclosed with affected outputs.

Data Protection & Privacy

Collect the minimum personal data necessary; do not publish private personal data as evidence.

Corrections & Retractions

Corrections preserve version history, describe impact and link superseded releases.

Authorship & Contribution

Credit follows documented contribution; advisory conversations are not authorship.

Accessibility

Core research information should remain readable without interactive JavaScript where feasible.

Data Licensing

Original Lab code/docs follow repository licensing; source-data rights remain with publishers.

Security

Security or vulnerability reports should be handled privately and remediated before unnecessary public detail.

See TRANSPARENCY.md for the underlying versioning and evidence principles.
