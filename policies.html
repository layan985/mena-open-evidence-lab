Institutional Policies

Minimum standards for research, data and commissioned work.

These policies operationalize the Lab’s transparency standard. They define how evidence is labeled, corrected, credited, protected and disclosed, and are revised as the Lab accumulates contributors, users and commissioned work.

Research Integrity

No evidence label exceeds the strongest documented validation state. Material errors trigger correction or retraction records.

Research Ethics

Human-subject or sensitive-data work requires an explicit ethical and legal basis before collection.

Conflicts of Interest

Material financial, personal and commissioning conflicts are disclosed with affected outputs.

Data Protection & Privacy

Collect the minimum personal data necessary; do not publish private personal data as evidence.

Corrections & Retractions

Corrections preserve version history, describe impact and link superseded releases. Retractions retain a public record of the reason and affected claims.

Authorship & Contribution

Credit follows documented contribution; advisory conversations are not authorship.

Accessibility

Core research information should remain readable without interactive JavaScript where feasible.

Data Licensing

Original Lab code and documentation follow repository licensing; source-data rights remain with publishers.

Security & Vulnerability Disclosure

Security or vulnerability reports are handled privately and remediated before unnecessary public technical detail is released.
