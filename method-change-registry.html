MENA Statistical Method Change Registry

A source-linked record of when an official statistical series changes concept, classification, survey design, sampling frame, compilation method or historical treatment — and what a data user should do about it.

Download registry JSON →Evidence provenance standard →

Why this exists

A time series can keep the same familiar label after the measurement system underneath it has changed. The registry treats the method version as part of the observation. It does not rank statistical agencies and does not interpret methodological change as a quality failure.

Saudi Arabia · National accounts

2023 comprehensive GDP revision

comprehensive revisionmethod regimesource expansion

GASTAT selected 2023 as the comprehensive revision year, expanded data coverage and updated methodology, and introduced chain linking for real GDP in 2024.

User action: preserve the release vintage. Pre- and post-revision estimates should not be treated as interchangeable when reconstructing what information was available in real time.
Jordan · National accounts

Quarterly GDP series comprehensively revised

historical revisionclassification change

Jordan's Department of Statistics states that the quarterly GDP series from 2008 through Q2 2025 underwent a new comprehensive revision. ISIC Rev.4 was adopted and sector detail expanded from 14 to 20 activities.

User action: record the method version when using historical quarterly GDP and do not splice archived pre-revision sector series into the revised series without an explicit bridge.
Morocco · Labour market

EMO2026 replaces the former ENE survey

concept changesurvey replacementsample redesign

Morocco's new labour-force survey changes the definition of employment and unemployment, expands labour-underutilization measurement, increases the annual sample from 90,000 to 135,000 households, updates the sampling frame using the 2024 census and introduces a new rotation design.

User action: HCP explicitly states that 2026 EMO results are not directly comparable with the old published ENE series. Use the EMO-compatible backcast indicators for 2017–2025 where available.

Contribution rule

A method-change record enters the public registry only when the producer documents the change or the Lab can trace it to a first-party methodological source. Each record must identify the affected series, effective period, documented change, comparability consequence and recommended user action.

Claim boundary

Method changes can improve measurement. The registry is designed to preserve statistical identity through time, not to score institutional competence.
