ENGAGEMENT 001

Regional Jobs & Resilience Evidence Review

Commissioned2026Confidential clientMENAActive

A commissioned evidence engagement involving source reconstruction, programme-data QA, causal inference, uncertainty analysis and decision support. Client identity, restricted data, client-specific results and sensitive recommendations remain confidential.

Decision question
How should a regional programme portfolio distinguish apparent performance from defensible impact when implementation quality, participant attrition, economic conditions and evidence quality differ materially across markets?
Scope

What the Lab was asked to own

  1. 01Reconstruct the evidentiary perimeter.
  2. 02Reconcile inconsistent measures and denominators.
  3. 03Establish provenance for material statistics.
  4. 04Evaluate selection and attrition risk.
  5. 05Separate descriptive performance from causal evidence.
  6. 06Stress-test programme comparisons.
  7. 07Integrate relevant country-level economic conditions.
  8. 08Identify claims that should be withheld.
  9. 09Design the final decision and allocation framework.

Public method note

Why the analytical architecture matters

Naïve completed-case difference+4.90 pp
Attrition-adjusted doubly robust estimate+6.77 pp
Known effect in controlled method rehearsal+6.81 pp
RANDOMIZED / SYNTHETIC METHOD REHEARSAL — NOT CLIENT PERFORMANCE DATA

Before applying the analytical pipeline to restricted client records, the Lab stress-tested the estimator on a controlled 180,000-record environment with confounding and informative attrition. The exercise tests whether the proposed method can recover a known effect under conditions resembling the identification problem. It is not evidence about the client's programme.

Evidence withheld

Some questions should end without a number.

The engagement architecture permits a ranking, impact estimate or allocation recommendation to be withheld where comparability, identification, denominator integrity or structural breaks make the result indefensible.
