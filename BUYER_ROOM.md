## Buyer-room standard

Every commissioned output should ship with, where applicable:

- executive brief;
- dataset or evidence table;
- data dictionary;
- source/provenance register;
- methodology;
- QA report;
- limitations register;
- reproducibility instructions;
- decision memo;
- chart pack;
- change log;
- exact evidence labels on material claims.

The Lab does not count automated checks as external review, does not turn source ambiguity into false precision, and does not describe an output as institutional use until an external institution actually uses it.
