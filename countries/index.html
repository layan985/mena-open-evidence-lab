What every priority room contains

The same seven evidence objects make country work inspectable rather than decorative.

01

Current dashboard

Six to eight indicators with value, reference period, release date, official source, vintage and definition. Values without a complete provenance chain are visibly withheld.

02

Official source map

Who produces prices, labour, monetary, fiscal, trade and national-accounts evidence, plus frequency, format and archive quality.

03

Measurement notes

Population perimeters, denominators, base years, survey redesigns, geographic scope and terminology that determine whether two numbers are actually comparable.

04

Release & revision ledger

Reference period, first release, current preserved state and revision flag: the base layer for reconstructing what was knowable at a historical date.

05

Original findings

At least two small, source-linked MENA Lab findings per room. Descriptive claims stay descriptive unless a causal design is documented.

06–07

Research program + status

Five named country work objects, machine-readable downloads where real files exist, and evidence-status counts based on actual records rather than fake completion percentages.
