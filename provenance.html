Research provenance graph

A claim should be able to walk backward to its evidence.

The Lab’s provenance contract connects source, retrieval time, vintage, raw evidence, transformations and code, versioned release, publication, outside scrutiny, corrections and archival state. A project stops at the last state it can actually support.

01Official / public source02Retrieval + timestamp03Vintage / revision state04Raw evidence05Transformation + code06Versioned dataset / release07Publication / claim08External review09Independent reproduction10Method disagreement11Correction / revision12Archive / DOI state
SOLID = production/version recordDASHED = outside scrutiny, only when supported

Why the graph matters

A polished publication page cannot compensate for a missing source lineage. Conversely, a well-documented dataset should remain useful even before it becomes a paper. The graph makes those dependencies inspectable and keeps review/reproduction from being inferred merely because the institution has aggregate external-validation activity.
