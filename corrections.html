Corrections archive

The absence of corrections is itself recorded, not implied.

Every material correction should identify the affected object/version, original state, corrected state, reason, discovery route, date, severity and whether conclusions changed.

Correction classes

Metadata

Link, date, citation or descriptive fields changed without altering underlying evidence.

Data

One or more observations, transformations or source mappings changed.

Method

Code, specification or analytical procedure changed.

Interpretation

Published language or conclusion changed because the evidence no longer supported the original wording.
