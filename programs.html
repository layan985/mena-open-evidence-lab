Research architecture · updated 21 August 2026

Five programmes. One measurement system.

The Lab now organizes research around five durable measurement problems. Existing projects remain, but every new project must strengthen one of these programmes and leave behind reusable data, methods or validation infrastructure.

Flagship build

MENA-RTD — Real-Time Data and Vintages Database

Reconstructing the information that actually existed at historical dates so revision analysis, release-latency research and pseudo-real-time nowcasting can be done without revised-history leakage.

Research standard

Try to kill the finding before publishing it.

Every material empirical conclusion must survive at least one deliberately hostile but defensible specification. Frozen inputs, provenance, uncertainty and failure modes are part of the result.

Cross-program infrastructure

MENA Evidence Terminal

Machine-readable observations preserve source, release type, language, file identity, vintage, transformation, code and validation state.
