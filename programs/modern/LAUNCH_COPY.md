# MODERN Cohort 01 — Public Launch Copy

## Short announcement

Most MENA research does not begin with a regression.

It begins with broken spreadsheets, changing institution names, ambiguous reporting periods, inaccessible PDFs, missing provenance, and a number nobody can trace back six months later.

**MODERN — the MENA Open Data, Evidence & Reproducibility Network — is recruiting its founding contributor cohort.**

We are building open, research-ready economic evidence across labor markets, firms + AI, financial markets, public policy, trade, climate, and institutional narratives.

This is not a membership club and participation is not awarded for attendance. Contributors are credited only after shipping reviewable public work.

The first intake is prioritizing five execution roles:
- Labor Markets
- Firms + AI
- Financial Markets
- Public Policy + Data
- Research Engineering

Applicants complete a small reproducibility task. We care more about careful provenance, code that runs, and honest limitations than university prestige.

**Priority application deadline: 22 August 2026.**

**Sunday, 23 August 2026**

## Researcher / faculty version

The MENA Open Data & Evidence Lab is launching MODERN, a production network for reproducible MENA economic research.

We are looking for researchers and practitioners willing to contribute in one of two ways:

1. **Bounded methodological review** — independently inspect one public research object using a short provenance/reproducibility QA packet.
2. **Research problem nomination** — identify one MENA dataset, measurement problem, or empirical question that is unnecessarily difficult to work with and could benefit from open research infrastructure.

We are deliberately not building an honorary advisory board. Reviewer credit is attached to a defined object and only made public with permission.
