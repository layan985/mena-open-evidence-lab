## What you will do
You will work from a synthetic panel dataset and reproduce a complete small empirical workflow:

1. inspect raw data
2. validate identifiers and panel structure
3. calculate descriptive statistics
4. estimate a simple difference-in-differences effect
5. reproduce a figure/table
6. compare output against an answer key
7. document exactly what another person needs to rerun your result
