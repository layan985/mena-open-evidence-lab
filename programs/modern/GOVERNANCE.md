# Governance

## Roles

### Research Director
Owns program strategy, external representation, final release approval, and conflict resolution.

### Research Coordinator
Maintains the release calendar, contributor registry, workshop operations, and partner follow-up.

### Pod Leads (7)
Own pod scope, assign work, review provenance, and approve contributions before central QA.

### MODERN Research Fellows (11)
Complete the structured training track and contribute to at least one validated public release.

### Research Contributors
Complete scoped research tasks and receive credit only for verifiable contributions.

### Faculty / Methodological Advisors
May review methods, datasets, workshop content, or project designs. Advisor status must never imply supervision that did not occur.

## Decisions
Routine pod decisions: Pod Lead. Release approval: Pod Lead + central QA. Methodological disputes: documented review by Research Director; external advisor consulted when material. Corrections and retractions require a public changelog entry.

## Authorship and credit
Credit follows contribution, not title. Dataset records should use CRediT-style contribution statements where possible.

## Research integrity
Fabrication, falsification, plagiarism, hidden manual edits to processed data, invented sources, or unverifiable provenance are grounds for removal from a release and possible removal from the cohort.

## Public claims
MODERN never reports a partner, user, release, contributor, workshop, or observation count without registry evidence.
