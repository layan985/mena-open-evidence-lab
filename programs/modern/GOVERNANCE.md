# Governance

The MENA Open Data & Evidence Lab is an independent research initiative. Research priorities and methodological standards are overseen by the Founder & Research Director, with contributions from independent researchers and collaborators. Outputs are developed under principles of transparency, reproducibility, documented correction, and open research.

## Roles

### Founder & Research Director

**Layan Oraidi** owns program strategy, research direction, external representation, repository stewardship, and founder-produced release approval. Founder approval does not count as independent review.

### Contributors

No contributor is counted from an application, title, meeting, or draft. A contributor is listed only after an attributable public contribution is accepted, such as a merged data/code change, completed audit, released teaching artifact, or substantive methods contribution.

### Research collaborators

Research collaborators may co-produce a defined dataset, method, audit, or workshop. A reply, introduction, informal conversation, or review request does not establish collaborator status.

**Current publicly confirmed research collaborators as of 13 August 2026: 0.**

### Operational roles

Dataset lead, data contributor, methods contributor, reproducibility reviewer, workshop facilitator, and release reviewer are role definitions, not staffed positions. Each role is attached to a named output and credited only when the work exists.

## Decisions
Founder-produced work may be approved for an alpha, pilot, or release-candidate label by the Research Director. Stable releases that claim independent reproduction require a non-producing reviewer. Methodological disputes, corrections, withdrawals, and retractions require a public record.

## Authorship and credit
Credit follows contribution, not title. Dataset records should use CRediT-style contribution statements where possible.

The producing analyst, independent reviewer, and adjudicator must be named separately when those functions are performed by different people. No person may audit their own work and be reported as an independent reproducer.

## Research integrity
Fabrication, falsification, plagiarism, hidden manual edits to processed data, invented sources, or unverifiable provenance are grounds for removal from a release and contributor status.

## Public claims
MODERN never reports a partner, user, release, contributor, workshop, or observation count without registry evidence.

FORM application activity is reported only as an application until membership is formally confirmed. A membership, if confirmed, does not imply FORM review or endorsement of Lab research.

## Corrections, licensing, and conflicts

- Material corrections produce a new version and changelog entry; released files are not silently overwritten.
- Repository code and original Lab materials use the repository's MIT License unless a file states otherwise.
- Source data remain subject to source-specific terms; a code license does not relicense third-party data.
- Financial, supervisory, organizational, or personal interests that could affect production or review must be disclosed in the relevant release or audit.
- Suspected errors should identify the release, stable record ID, source evidence, and expected correction through the public repository issue route.
