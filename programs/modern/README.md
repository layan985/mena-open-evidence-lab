# MODERN — MENA Open Data, Evidence & Reproducibility Network

MODERN is a production network inside the MENA Open Data & Evidence Lab. It is designed around verifiable outputs, not membership counts.

## Cohort 01
**Period:** August 2026–February 8, 2027  
**Model:** 7 university pods + central research team  
**Core rule:** participation is counted only when a contributor produces reviewable research work.

## Pods
1. Labor Markets
2. Firms + AI
3. Trade + Industrial Policy
4. Water + Climate
5. Public Finance
6. Financial Markets
7. Narratives + Institutions

Each pod is responsible for two public dataset releases. The central team is responsible for four cross-pod releases, producing a total target of 18 open datasets.
