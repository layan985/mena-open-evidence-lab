# MODERN Contributor Selection Rubric

Applications are scored on work evidence and fit, not prestige or institutional brand.

## Score (20 points)

### 1. Reproducibility task — 0–8
- 0: missing / not reproducible
- 2: source cited but workflow incomplete
- 4: basic raw-to-clean script and README work
- 6: reproducible pipeline with clear transformations and sensible validation
- 8: excellent provenance, clean code, validation, interpretation, and reproducibility

### 2. Research judgment — 0–4
Does the applicant ask a tractable, meaningful question and recognize limitations?

### 3. Reliability signals — 0–3
Evidence of finishing work, documenting it, responding to feedback, or maintaining projects.

### 4. Pod fit — 0–3
Skills/interests match an active production need.

### 5. Communication — 0–2
Clear explanation without inflated claims.

## Selection principles
- Do not use university prestige as a scoring category.
- Do not collect unnecessary sensitive data.
- Prefer demonstrated execution over self-rated skill.
- Admit fewer people if reviewer capacity is insufficient.
- A fellowship place requires stronger evidence than general contributor status.

## Fellowship threshold
Suggested: >=16/20 plus successful interview or asynchronous methods review.

## Contributor threshold
Suggested: >=12/20, subject to pod capacity.
