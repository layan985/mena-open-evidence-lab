MENA Statistical Memory

Official data portals are very good at telling you what the number is now. This project preserves what the number said then — and the exact moment the historical story changed.

Contraction became growth

The official 2023 real-GDP estimate moved by +1.3 percentage points after the comprehensive national-accounts revision. This is classified as a sign reversal and a method-regime change.

What changed?

The historical economic narrative changed even though the reference year did not.

GASTAT documents a comprehensive revision using richer survey and administrative data, supply-and-use work, updated classifications and historical backcasting.

This project does not interpret revision size as institutional weakness. Revisions can reflect better evidence and better methods.

The research question

What did the official data say at the time an analyst, journalist, researcher or policymaker actually had to make a decision — and how different is that information from the final historical series we see today?

That distinction matters because models evaluated on final data can look better than they would have in real time, historical narratives can silently change, and old analysis can become impossible to reproduce if the earlier vintage disappears.

What the ledger stores

Each observation records the country, indicator, reference period, release date, vintage, value, status, method version and primary source. Revision events then link two vintages and classify what changed: sign reversal, threshold reversal, ranking reversal, acceleration reversal, level revision, method-regime change or correction.

What comes next

The first release will build a 25-event pilot across Saudi Arabia, Jordan and Morocco, beginning with national accounts, labour-market headline measures and CPI. The next target is a 100-event regional ledger with Egypt, Tunisia, UAE, Qatar, Bahrain, Oman and Kuwait.

Contribute a missing vintage

If you work with MENA official statistics and have an older release, methodology notice, archived table, revision event or broken historical comparison that should be preserved, send it to menaevidencelab@gmail.com. Every accepted event will keep the original source and review trail.
