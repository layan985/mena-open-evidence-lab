# MENA Shock Transmission Atlas — Research Specification v0.1

Released: 22 August 2026

## Question

When a regional or global shock hits, through which measurable channels is each MENA economy exposed, and which channels are large enough to matter for the interpretation of subsequent macroeconomic data?

## Country exposure vector

The Atlas will preserve separate channels rather than manufacture a single opaque vulnerability score:

- hydrocarbon export dependence
- hydrocarbon import dependence
- food import dependence
- tourism receipts exposure
- remittance inflow dependence
- freight / shipping exposure
- fiscal space indicators
- external financing need
- reserve / import-cover indicators where methodologically comparable
- trade concentration and major partner exposure

## Time discipline

Every exposure vector is dated. A 2026 shock cannot be analyzed with a 2028 exposure structure. Historical analysis must use information and weights applicable to the shock date, with vintage state recorded where possible.

## Identification discipline

The Atlas is initially descriptive infrastructure. Exposure is not causal effect. Event studies, difference-in-differences or synthetic-control designs are added only where treatment timing, counterfactual construction and pre-trend evidence support them.

## Planned outputs

1. source-linked country exposure vectors;
2. shock-specific transmission maps;
3. release-timeline overlays showing when effects become visible in official data;
4. sensitivity analysis across alternative exposure definitions;
5. explicit missing-data and comparability register.

## Claim boundary

Version 0.1 releases the research design only. No country vulnerability ranking or causal shock effect is claimed until the source-linked panel is built and validated.
