# MENA Open Data & Evidence Lab

Open, reproducible research infrastructure for economic and social evidence across the Middle East and North Africa.

## MODERN

**MODERN — MENA Open Data, Evidence & Reproducibility Network** is the Lab's six-month contributor and training program. Cohort 01 runs from August 2026 to February 8, 2027.

### Cohort 01 targets

- 23 contributors from 7 universities
- 18 open datasets released
- 42,000+ observations standardized
- 6 reproducibility workshops
- 11 undergraduate researchers trained
- 3 institutional research partners
- 2 policy organizations using MODERN data

See [`programs/modern/`](programs/modern/) for the operating system, release standard, fellowship, workshops, partnerships, policy-use protocol, and live metric definitions.
