# MENA Open Data & Evidence Lab

**Open, reproducible research infrastructure for economic and social evidence across the Middle East and North Africa.**

The project has three layers under one brand:

| Layer | Name | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Institution | **MENA Open Data & Evidence Lab** | Governance, standards, publication, partnerships, research direction |
| Humans | **MODERN — MENA Open Data, Evidence & Reproducibility Network** | Contributor network, fellowship, seven research pods, workshops |
| Product | **MENA Evidence Terminal** | Searchable evidence catalog, provenance, revisions, APIs and research workflows |

**Lab = institution. MODERN = humans. Terminal = infrastructure.**

The root of this repository now contains the public site and Terminal schema prototype. Prototype records do not fabricate statistics: a field remains visibly unavailable until a validated release supports it.

## Anchor releases

The Lab will prioritize three serious anchor objects before expanding the catalog:

1. **J-CLIMA / Jordan Corporate Lineage** — auditable longitudinal corporate identities, changing identifiers and entity relationships.
2. **MENA Policy Shock & Event Database** — machine-readable policy events with source documents, dates, coding decisions and revision histories.
3. **MENA Economic Narrative Corpus** — institutional language linked reproducibly to economic series and document provenance.

Each anchor is intended to ship with an API, DOI, methodology paper, provenance graph and reproducibility package. These are plans until a release passes the public validation standard.

## MODERN Cohort 01

**Fellowship size:** 11 fellows.

**Public calendar:**
- August 10, 2026 — announcement
- August 11–22 — applications + technical task
- August 23–25 — shortlist review
- August 27 — founding cohort announced
- August 29 — orientation
- September 1 — first production sprint

A fellow graduates only after all four requirements are satisfied:
1. Complete at least 5 of 6 core workshops.
2. Make at least one substantive, reviewable contribution to a validated public release.
3. Complete an independent replication or reproducibility audit.
4. Submit a final research memo covering methods, contribution, limitations and lessons.

Attendance alone earns no completion credential.

See [`programs/modern/`](programs/modern/) for governance, fellowship rules, dataset standards, release operations, workshops and metric definitions.

## Public scoreboard

The homepage starts at **zero** for external contributors, universities represented, validated releases, independent reproductions, outside API users, institutional partners, policy uses, citations and workshop graduates.

Numbers move only when public registry evidence supports them. Intentions, conversations, applications and unmerged work do not count.

## Twelve-month target — August 2027

Target, not current claim: **7 universities; 25–40 validated contributors; 12–18 serious DOI-backed releases; 100k+ standardized observations; 10 independent reproducibility audits; 6 workshops; 3 faculty advisers; 3 institutional collaborations; 2 documented policy/research uses; one external code maintainer; one conference presentation; one methods/data paper; one public API with outside users.**
