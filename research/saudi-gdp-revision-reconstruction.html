How much of Saudi economic history changed after the comprehensive revision?

This project reconstructs annual real-GDP growth for 2011–2023 using two official GASTAT vintages: one published before the 2023 comprehensive national-accounts revision and one after it. The goal is to measure the revision year by year and identify qualitative narrative reversals without filling gaps from secondary databases.

2023 real GDP growth: −0.8% → +0.5%

The revision is +1.3 percentage points and crosses zero. That changes the qualitative description of the year from contraction to growth. This is a change in the official statistical record, not a retroactive change in underlying 2023 economic activity.

Acquisition rule

The reconstruction uses official vintages as evidence objects, not a latest-vintage database with historical values copied backward.

01 · Identify

Freeze the two GASTAT workbooks

The pre-revision workbook is identified as Real_Gross_Domestic_Product_(2).xlsx_fixed_2647169. The post-revision workbook is identified as Real_Gross_Domestic_Product_(4).xlsx_fixed_3036341.

02 · Archive

Hash the original files

Both workbooks must be archived byte-for-byte and hashed before annual cells are admitted into the public reconstruction.

03 · Extract

Normalize 2011–2023

Only directly extracted annual real-GDP-growth cells enter the normalized input table. Search snippets, chart reading and secondary databases are excluded.
