Independent methods review capacity

Reviewers are separate from the primary production role. Release-level pages identify a reviewer only where the specific review artifact and attribution can be disclosed.

Profile standard

A full person profile may contain name, role, research areas, bio, education or affiliations, selected research, datasets, software, projects, publications, events, ORCID, GitHub, Scholar and contact—but only where each field is accurate and public.

Disclosure rule

The institutional metrics ledger may report aggregate people counts even when individual identities are confidential or not yet cleared for publication. Aggregate totals never imply a named person performed a specific review unless the corresponding release-level record says so.
