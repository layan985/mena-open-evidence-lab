Named only when the work is real.

Leadership, contributors and advisors are separate categories. Vacant roles remain visible as vacant. No person is listed as staff, advisor or contributor before explicit acceptance or attributable work exists.

Credit rule

Contributor credit follows accepted, attributable work. Advisor status follows explicit acceptance. Organization size is never inferred from applicants, prospects, one-off conversations or informal interest.
